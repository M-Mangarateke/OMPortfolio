About Oreneile Machitje

I’m Oreneile Machitje, an AFDA Motion Picture graduate specialising in directing and visual effects. My portfolio includes the short film Burn Mouth, a VFX showreel and short-form edits across beauty, fashion and lifestyle.

My background has equipped me with a solid understanding of narrative development, shot composition, working with performers, and integrating visual effects into story-driven projects. I am comfortable working in collaborative environments and bringing a flexible, detail-oriented, and creative approach to every production.

I am seeking opportunities within the film and creative industries where I can continue to grow, contribute meaningfully, and develop impactful visual work.
